# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

NYC parents (35–55, often first-generation or immigrant families, bilingual English/中文 segment) deciding whether to invest in SAT or SHSAT preparation for their student. They arrive from Meta/Google ads onto the VSL funnel pages (/sat, /shsat) skeptical of tutoring-industry hype and protective of their money and their child's time. The job to be done: "tell me honestly where my student stands, and whether this company is credible enough to trust with a four-figure investment."

## Product Purpose

IvyPath Academy (Perevalis Tutoring LLC) sells premium SAT/SHSAT test prep and college consulting, taught by 99th-percentile tutors currently at Ivy League and Top 20 universities. The funnel pages exist to convert paid traffic into free-diagnostic starts and consultation bookings. Success = a parent watches the founder's video, believes the proof, and starts the free diagnostic.

## Brand Personality

"The Elite Mentor": confident, results-anchored, parent-addressed, investment-framed. Warm old-money collegiate, not corporate edtech. Calm authority — never hype, never pressure.

**Hard language rules (from IvyPath Source of Truth v1.5):**
- "student", never "kid(s)"; "investment", never cost/fee/price; "tutor/mentor", never teacher/instructor; "consultation", never sales call
- Banned words: discount, cheap, affordable, course, class
- Never promise a specific score or point gain in copy. The conditional "Score Improvement Guarantee" program may be named and its terms described.
- Say "Ivy League and Top 20" (not "Ivy League tutors" alone)
- Approved claims only: CMU/Cornell/Harvard/Princeton acceptances, Stuyvesant offer (SHSAT 606), SAT 1210→1530 and 1370→1500, 7× AP 5, 96% pass rate, 99th-percentile tutors, bilingual English/中文, automated parent session reports.
- **Consulting claims (user-authorized 2026-07-06, written consent + proof docs on file):** client placements at Brown, Carnegie Mellon, Princeton, Harvard; anonymized transformation case study (1000 SAT → 1400, Stanford neurology internship, NYC ecology internship, eight APs, "shared with permission" + "Results vary" required); consultants named as Alp (Cornell) and Edison Zhu (Princeton), 2+ years experience; two-track pricing (Foundation $3,500/$6,000/$10,500 per year; Application $5,500/$8,500/$14,500) is public. Never: admission guarantees; copy asserting the reader's own child's deficiency.

## Anti-references

- Generic edtech SaaS (blue/purple gradients, rounded cards with icons)
- Kumon/test-prep-mill visual language (bright primaries, clipart, urgency banners)
- Hype-marketing VSL pages (red countdown timers, fake scarcity, ALL-CAPS promises)
- Shopify-template e-commerce feel

## Design Principles

1. **Proof over promises** — real score reports, real offers, real letters carry the page; copy stays factual. (Ad data: the "real Stuyvesant offer" creative converts at $6.47/lead; generic promises don't.)
2. **Old-money collegiate warmth** — dark-academia photography, serif confidence, gold used sparingly like a wax seal.
3. **A parent's skepticism is the design constraint** — every section answers an objection; disclaimers visible, terms honest.
4. **One action** — start the free diagnostic. Consultation is the soft alternative, phone is the fallback. Nothing else competes.
5. **The founder is the brand** — Benjamin (Stuyvesant '20, 1570 SAT, Cornell) speaking directly to parents is the trust engine; production quality must match the promise of "elite."

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1 on cream. Captions burned into VSL videos (majority of paid-traffic viewers watch muted). prefers-reduced-motion respected. Content must never be gated behind scroll-reveal animation. Bilingual support surfaced (English/中文).
