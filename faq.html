Everything You Need to Know

All of our tutors are current students or recent graduates of Ivy League and top-tier universities. They scored in the 99th percentile on the exams they teach and have undergone our rigorous vetting and training process.

We offer both online and in-person sessions across New York City. Online sessions are conducted via Zoom with screen sharing and digital whiteboards. In-person sessions can be arranged at public libraries or your home.

Standard sessions are 60 minutes. For intensive test prep, we also offer 90-minute and 2-hour sessions. Session length can be customized to fit your student's needs and attention span.
